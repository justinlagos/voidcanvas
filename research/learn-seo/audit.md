# Learn audit, 27 September 2026

Scope: everything under `/learn` on voidcanvas.netlify.app and the code behind it (`src/content/learn/*`, `src/app/(site)/learn/*`, `src/components/site/*`, `src/app/sitemap.ts`, `src/app/robots.ts`). Data: `audit-data.json` (generated by `audit-articles.mjs`) and the live HTML.

## What is there

- 74 articles, 68,623 words, 314 minutes of reading. 44 Beginner, 30 Intermediate, 0 Advanced.
- 8 software-first categories: Start here 9, Editor 26, Studio 9, Effects 7, Workflows 7, Design craft 7, Reference 4, Help 5.
- 4 learning paths (New to Voidcanvas, Coming from Photoshop, Client work with Studio, Designing for print). 49 of 74 articles are in no path, so the hub's "Pick a path" only promotes 25 articles.
- Content is typed TypeScript data (`Block[]`), rendered on the server. Articles ship almost no JavaScript. This is a real asset: it means search, schema and new block types can be added without touching prose.
- Every article has `keywords`, `related` (2 to 4) and a one-paragraph summary. No broken internal links, no broken `related` slugs.
- Accuracy: the writer brief demanded that every label, shortcut and number be checked against the code. Spot checks (masks, export for print, designing for print) confirm the tone: honest about limits (RGB only PDF, image-based PDF, 96 dpi for small presets).

## What is wrong or missing

### Discovery
1. The hub is documentation-first. Hero says "Learn Voidcanvas. Learn design." then lists categories named after modules (Editor, Studio, Effects). A person arriving with "my print came out blurry" has to know that the answer lives under Design craft > Image resolution explained.
2. Search is substring AND-matching over title, summary, headings and keywords. Every typed word must appear somewhere. "blurry image" fails unless both words are present; "photoshop masks" fails because "photoshop" appears in almost no article. No synonyms, no stemming, no problem phrases. Results show title, summary and category only.
3. No goal-based entry points. Nothing answers "I want to make a poster" or "I want to edit a PSD" as a route.
4. 27 articles have no `try` link into the product. 2 articles have zero inbound links (workflow-event-poster, glossary). 4 have one or fewer.
5. Prev/Next at the bottom of an article are category siblings in file order, not a reading order. "Read next" is the `related` list dumped as cards with no reason given.

### Article page
6. No answer-first layer. The first block is always a paragraph (40 to 45 words) saying what the reader will be able to do. Good, but a person from Google wants the answer in the first screen.
7. 18 articles have no "Common problems" or "Good to know" section.
8. No images, diagrams or interactive examples anywhere in Learn (0 `<img>` on the live article). Effects and quick tools are described in words only, while `public/landing/fx-*.jpg` and the effect code itself are available.
9. The sidebar CTA is always "Open the Editor", even on Studio and Effects articles.

### SEO and metadata
10. Title tags: `${title} · Learn Voidcanvas`. 36 of 74 exceed 60 characters (longest 85). Google truncates these.
11. Meta descriptions: 19 summaries exceed 160 characters.
12. No `og:image`, no `twitter:card` larger than `summary`, no `og:site_name`. Shared links show no picture.
13. Schema: TechArticle with headline, description, dateModified, proficiencyLevel, publisher. No `datePublished`, no `author`, no `image`, no `mainEntityOfPage`. BreadcrumbList item 2 points to a fragment (`/learn#editor`), which is not a page.
14. No FAQPage schema anywhere, including the quick tools where FAQs already exist in the data.
15. Sitemap is correct (87 URLs, lastmod from `updated`), robots is fine. `keywords` meta tag is emitted (harmless, ignored by Google).
16. No category or goal hub pages exist as URLs. Everything hangs off `/learn` and `/learn/<slug>`.
17. The hub HTML is 323 KB because the whole search index (74 entries with all heading and keyword terms) is serialised into the page for the client search. Not a ranking problem, but it can be trimmed.

### Content shape against what people search
18. Titles are task-shaped ("Hide and show parts of a layer with masks") rather than query-shaped ("How to mask a layer"). Fine for readers already in the product; weak for search where the query is "how to make a halftone", "how to edit a psd without photoshop", "how to prepare a poster for print".
19. The library is strongest on product documentation and honest limits. It is thin on the problem-shaped questions that bring people from search: blurry prints, PSD without Photoshop, brand consistency, poster sizes, Canva and Photoshop alternatives, working offline, privacy of photo editors. Those are exactly where Voidcanvas has a real answer.

## Decisions per article

Retain (as is, with new metadata): all 74. Nothing here is wrong; the problem is the layer around it.

Reclassify into goals (a new axis, not a rename): every article is tagged with one or more goals (make a poster, prepare for print, edit a photo, build a brand, social content, work with PSD, design effects, run a client project, one design many formats, learn design, work faster, leave Photoshop, private and offline).

Turn into cornerstone (already carries the weight, needs the answer layer and inbound links): designing-for-print, image-resolution-explained, building-a-brand-identity, import-psd-and-pdf, remove-background, resize-to-every-format, studio-overview, effects-overview, typography-fundamentals, colour-that-works.

Add as new query-shaped cornerstones (new articles, each linking down into the existing ones so nothing is duplicated): how to prepare a poster for print, how to make a halftone portrait, how to edit a PSD without Photoshop. Roadmap lists the rest.

Expand (short for the weight they carry): quick-tools (446 words for three tools people search for by name), teams (347), desktop-app (404), brand-kit (476). Not done in this pass except through the new halftone article, which quick-tools now points to.

Merge: none. install-as-an-app and desktop-app look similar but cover the PWA and the Electron app respectively; keep both, cross-link.

Redirect: none needed. No slugs change.

Rewrite titles for search: not done in bulk, on purpose. The titles are honest and the slugs are indexed. Instead each article gains `answers` phrases (the exact queries it answers) that feed search and the goal pages, and title tags are shortened at the template level.

## Mobile and performance

- The article page is server-rendered, single column under 1024 px, TOC collapses into a details element. Reads well at 375 px.
- Hub at 375 px: search is full width, path cards stack. Fine.
- Fonts are self-hosted by next/font. No layout shift observed.
- Article HTML ~113 KB with 15 script tags (Next runtime plus the small client islands: search, path nav, helpful, toc). Adding interactive demos must be done with dynamic imports so text-only articles stay this light.
