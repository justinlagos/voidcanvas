# Deep product UX audit: the small things

Date: 26 Sept 2026. Source: Justin's brief ("VoidCanvas: deep product UX audit") plus a walk through the production build on master (ef57eda) at 1440 px and on an iPhone 13 viewport, driving the real workflows with Playwright: logo upload (transparent PNG, JPG on white, white-only PNG), brand build, guideline pages, save as client brand, job with brand, key visual in the Editor, photo plus logo, export, delivery, phone.

Standard: a working designer thinks "it already handled that". Every finding below is something the software could reasonably know.

Priority: P0 painful now, P1 real professional workflow gain, P2 quality of life, P3 later intelligence. Order within a group is by frequency × frustration × importance × leverage.

## Brand

### B1. Logo contrast is measured from the wrong pixels (P0)
- Problem: `analyseLogo` buckets colours along the mark's outer edge with alpha > 200. After a flat background is knocked out (a JPG on white), the outermost opaque pixels are the anti-aliased ones, blended with the old background.
- Why it matters: the number drives every recommendation on the Logo page and in the Editor. A wrong number is worse than none.
- Current: JPG Kobo logo (dark ink text plus blue disc): "light surface 3.17:1", "dark surface full colour 4.66:1". The dark text on the near-black surface is invisible, and the page recommends it.
- Better: cluster the mark's solid interior pixels (alpha ≥ 250, at least two pixels from any transparent neighbour), weight by area, and take the worst contrast among colours that make up at least 8% of the mark. Anti-aliasing and thin outlines never vote.
- Approach: `src/lib/intelligence/asset.ts` (profile) replaces the edge buckets; `markContrast` reads the profile.

### B2. "Reversed white" fakes a variant on multicolour marks (P0)
- Problem: `monoMark` fills the whole silhouette one colour. A mark whose meaning lives in the boundary between two colours (an orange dot inside a blue disc) becomes a blob.
- Current: the builder shows a white disc labelled "Reversed white" as the recommended treatment on brand and dark surfaces.
- Better: generate a knockout only when it is visually valid. Measure the share of internal colour boundaries (opaque pixels whose neighbour is a different colour cluster) against the outline. If internal edges carry the mark, a flat knockout is not safe: say so, keep full colour on a holding shape, and ask for the client's reversed artwork. Offer tonal (greyscale) variants, which keep the boundaries, and mono only when they hold.
- Approach: `intelligence/logo.ts` `deriveVariants(profile)` with `valid` and `reason` per variant; the builder and the client brand store what was accepted.

### B3. Client brands know nothing about the logo (P0)
- Problem: `ClientBrand.logos` is a list of blobs with a manual On light / On dark toggle. Rules are two numbers with no source. The guideline builder's analysis is thrown away on save.
- Why it matters: the Editor reads the client brand, not the builder. Everything the builder learns has to live in the brand to be used later.
- Better: the brand carries a logo system: variants (primary, reversed, mono dark, mono light, greyscale) with `derivedFrom` and a serialisable profile, and rules with a source (`suggested` from the artwork, `designer` when set by hand). The builder's save carries them. The Brands view shows and edits them.
- Approach: extend `ClientBrand` (`logoRules`, `logos[].variant`, `logos[].profile`, `logos[].derivedFrom`); `intelligence/brand.ts` builds the system from a profile; migration is additive.

### B4. Backgrounds tested are four abstract swatches (P1)
- Better: white, black, brand, secondary, accent, darkest and lightest brand tints, neutral grey, a light and a dark photograph, a busy texture. Each cell shows the chosen treatment and a grade: good, worth checking, needs attention, with one line of why ("the ink text drops to 1.3:1 here").
- Approach: `intelligence/backgrounds.ts` `testBackgrounds(profile, variants, brandColours)`; the Logo page gets a "Backgrounds" layout with the full grid.

### B5. Logo on photography gets no help (P1)
- Better: given a photo, score the corners and centre for luminance, busyness (local variance) and subject overlap, choose the least-destructive fix in order: swap treatment, move placement, scrim (lowest opacity that clears 3:1), then blur.
- Approach: `intelligence/photo.ts` `placeLogoOnPhoto(photo, profile, variants)`; used by the guideline "In use" page and the Editor check.

### B6. Clear space is visual but has no wrong example; minimum size is a number (P1)
- Better: the clear-space page shows Correct (mark plus x) and Incorrect (a headline crowding it). The minimum-size page renders the mark at the minimum and at half, and says when strokes drop below one device pixel ("at 16 px the thinnest strokes are 0.6 px; consider a simplified mark"). Both labelled Suggested until the designer changes them.
- Approach: profile carries `minStroke` (fraction of height) from a distance-transform estimate; `pageClearSpace` gains the incorrect panel; `pageMinSize` new.

### B7. No misuse page (P1)
- Better: a page generated from the actual logo: stretched, squashed, rotated, recoloured, low contrast, cropped, crowded, drop shadow, on busy photography.
- Approach: new page kind `misuse` in `brand-pages.ts`.

### B8. Brand colour is set from the logo with no say (P2)
- Current: adding a logo overwrites the brand colour with the first chromatic palette colour and unlocks a new secondary/accent take. The designer finds out on the Colour tab.
- Better: suggest, don't set: "Brand colour from the logo: #0B3D91 [Use it]". Keep the old behaviour only when the brand colour is still the default.

### B9. Brand health is a diagnostics popover of contrast pairs (P2)
- Better: a quiet grouped summary (Logo, Colours, Typography, Rules) with ticks and "worth checking" lines, in the builder header popover and on the Brands view. Nothing red unless it fails.
- Approach: `intelligence/health.ts` `brandHealth(brand)`.

### B10. Uploaded assets keep their file names (P2)
- Better: suggest `Logo / Primary`, `Logo / Reversed`, `Logo / Wordmark / Primary` from the profile and file-name hints (white, rev, mono, icon, horizontal); the designer can change it.
- Approach: `intelligence/naming.ts`.

### B11. New take can reinvent the brand (P2)
- Better: two takes. Variation keeps colours, fonts and scale and only changes layout tokens and page layouts. Mutation is today's behaviour.
- Approach: `newTake('layout' | 'all')`.

### B12. Colour roles are fixed to brand/secondary/accent (P3)
- Better: suggest Surface, Background, Text, Success roles from the palette as suggested roles the designer approves. Partly there (surfaces, semantic) but not editable.

### B13. Type scale shows sizes, not use (P3)
- Better: hero, poster, paragraph, caption, button and quote set in the scale, so the system can be judged.

## Editor

### E1. A placed logo is full-width and centred (P0)
- Current: any image is fitted to the board and centred, including a 900 × 300 logo on a 1080 × 1350 post, over the photo's subject.
- Better: a brand logo goes in at 22% of the shorter side into a safe corner (top left, or the emptiest corner when a photo is there), with role `logo`, name `Logo · Primary`, clear space respected.
- Approach: `addImage` gains a `placement` option; the Add menu offers the job's brand logos with variants.

### E2. The job's brand logos are not in the Add menu (P0)
- Current: "Your logos" reads the Editor brand kit; a job's brand is only checked, never offered.
- Better: with `doc.brandId`, list that brand's logos and variants first.

### E3. No logo contrast check against what is behind it (P1)
- Better: sample the rendered pixels under the logo's bounds (layers below it), measure the mark's colours against the dominant tone, and say "This logo may lose contrast here. Try Reversed [Use reversed]", which swaps the pixels for the variant at the same size and place. Remember a dismissal per layer.
- Approach: `brandReport` gains `contrast` findings using `renderDoc` with a region.

### E4. Clear-space check flags the picture the logo sits on (P1)
- Current: a photo covering 25% of the board counts as crowding when the logo is on top of it.
- Better: a layer that fully contains the logo's zone is its background, not a neighbour.

### E5. Minimum size wording pretends to be a rule (P2)
- Better: "Below the recommended minimum (40 px at 1080)" and "Suggested" or "Set by you" from the rule's source.

### E6. Layer names for images (P2)
- Current: file name, which is often `IMG_3948`.
- Better: when the file name is a camera or screenshot name, use `Image · Portrait` / `Image · Landscape` / `Image · Square`, and `Logo · Primary` for brand logos.

### E7. Effects have no starting points (P2)
- Better: presets per effect family (Subtle, Editorial, Poster, Heavy) written as parameter sets, so the designer starts from the look, not the number.

### E8. Missing fonts default to Inter (P1)
- Current: every missing family is replaced by Inter unless the designer picks.
- Better: classify the missing family by name (serif, slab, sans, geometric, condensed, display, script, mono; weight words) and offer Closest match, Safer fallback and Use a font file.
- Approach: `intelligence/fonts.ts` with a classification table for the bundled families and name heuristics.

## Studio

### S1. Delivery has no readiness check (P1)
- Better: before Build the package: unbuilt formats, print formats without a PDF, latest version not approved, unresolved client pins, a review link still open, duplicate file names. Shown as "Ready to deliver" or "3 things need attention", each with a jump.
- Approach: `intelligence/preflight.ts` `deliveryPreflight(job, design, versions)`.

### S2. Brief completeness (P2)
- Better: after reading the brief, say what is missing: no date, no CTA, no logo mentioned, no dimensions or formats, dates that conflict.

### S3. Versions are numbers (P3)
- Better: semantic states (Direction A, Client revision, Approved, Final print). Labels exist; states do not.

## Export

### X1. Format choice has no destination (P1)
- Current: PNG / JPG / WEBP / PDF plus a scale.
- Better: PNG · Social (2×, no transparency), PNG · Transparent, JPG · Web (1×, 85%), PDF · Print, PDF · Client proof (small). Advanced controls stay.

### X2. No preflight before export (P1)
- Better: a quiet list above the button: images below 150 ppi on print boards ("the photo is 640 px wide and prints at 297 mm: about 55 ppi, it will look soft"), empty text layers, hidden layers, text running off the board, off-brand colours when a brand is set. Levels: good, worth checking, needs attention. Never blocks.

### X3. Errors say "failed" (P2)
- Current: "Export failed. Try a smaller size."
- Better: say what was too big and what will work ("4 boards at 2× is 27,000 px wide; export at 1× or fewer boards").

## Mobile

### M1. Brand guideline builder unusable on a phone (P0)
- Current: below `lg` the page keeps the desktop height constraint; the preview and page strip collapse to 0 px and paint over the form. Measured: section 0 px tall at y 155, aside 1038 px, page cannot scroll past 844.
- Better: on a phone the page is one scrolling column: preview, page strip, controls.
- Approach: drop `min-h-0` and `flex-1` below `lg` in `BrandGuideline.tsx`.

### M2. Brands view colour rows overflow (P2)
- Current: three-column grid with fixed-width inputs overlaps at 1440 and below.
- Better: two columns, inputs that shrink.

## The intelligence layer

One folder, `src/lib/intelligence/`, deterministic, unit tested, no React:

- `asset.ts`: `profileImage(canvas)` → transparency, artwork bounds, aspect, coverage, luminance, colour clusters with share, mono/multi, tone, kind guess (wordmark, mark, lockup), minimum stroke estimate, internal-edge share.
- `contrast.ts`: WCAG ratio, `markContrast(profile, bg)`, grades and explanations for marks (3:1 non-text rule, 4.5:1 for text-like marks).
- `logo.ts`: variants with validity.
- `backgrounds.ts`: the background test set and results.
- `photo.ts`: placement scoring on photography and least-destructive fixes.
- `naming.ts`: suggested asset and layer names.
- `fonts.ts`: font classification and replacement suggestions.
- `health.ts`: brand health.
- `preflight.ts`: export and delivery checks.

Language rule: every value is labelled Detected, Suggested, Recommended or Set by you. Nothing claims to be the brand's official rule unless the designer set it.

## Build order

1. Intelligence layer with tests (asset, contrast, logo variants, backgrounds, naming, fonts, preflight).
2. Brand model: rules with sources, variants; builder save; Brands view.
3. Builder: fixed contrast, variants with validity, background grid, incorrect clear-space, minimum size, misuse page, health, suggested brand colour, Variation vs Mutation.
4. Phone layout fix.
5. Editor: brand logos in Add menu, sensible placement, contrast check with Use reversed, clear-space fix, names.
6. Export: destination presets and preflight. Delivery: readiness.
7. Missing fonts: suggestions.
8. Effects presets. Empty states.
